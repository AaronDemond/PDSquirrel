Premium PD Squirrel membership – For a monthly fee of $29.99 (GST/HST included), you are entitled to take any 8 PD sessions during the month – you choose the sessions. The monthly fee is billed directly to your credit card on the first day of each monthly cycle. A cycle starts on the day you become a Premium member and ends on the same day of the following month, or the last day of the following month if there are fewer days in that month. If you do not take all 8 PD sessions in a monthly cycle, the unused entitlement is not carried over to the next month. You may purchase additional sessions, beyond the 8 included in your Premium membership, at the listed price.

Standard PD Squirrel membership – You purchase each PD session individually for the listed price. You pay no monthly fee.

Your PD is always available to you for at least 2 years – Every PD session taken by you (whether as a Premium member or a Standard member) is automatically saved for you in your PD Library. You can access these sessions at any time, whether you are a Premium member or Standard member.
