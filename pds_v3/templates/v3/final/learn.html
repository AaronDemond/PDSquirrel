Pricing

Join PD Squirrel today for free with just your name and email. You receive one free session credit when you join PD Squirrel for the first time.

Every new PD Squirrel member automatically becomes a Standard member. You may upgrade to Premium membership at any time with a few clicks.

Standard PD Squirrel member – No monthly fee. You pay only for each session you take. Our prices include any applicable GST/HST. Any PD session you take is kept in your personal PD library for unlimited future access.

Premium PD Squirrel member – For a monthly fee of just $29.99 (GST/HST included), you are entitled to take your choice of any eight (8) sessions during the monthly cycle. Your monthly cycle starts on the day you become a premium member and runs to the same day of the following month. Any PD session you take is kept in your personal PD library for unlimited future access. You may convert back to Standard membership at any time without penalty and without losing the PD sessions from your personal library.

Both Standard and Premium members have access to our report generator to help you keep track of your PD.
