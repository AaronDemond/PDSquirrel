PD Squirrel is all about providing an excellent platform to facilitate professionals developing professionals.

Presenters create and manage PD sessions easily from the Presenter Hub – here's how:

Create, Upload, Earn.

You create and upload. As a presenter, you provide an audio recording of your PD session directly to PD Squirrel. If needed, we will provide recording hardware and software to assist.

You review and release. You have the ability to preview and edit your session, from the Presenter Hub, before releasing it to us.

We accept and list. Once accepted, we list your session on PD Squirrel where it is immediately available to all members for purchase.

You Earn. You share in the revenue paid by members. More than half of all revenue is paid to our Presenters in the form of royalties (more). You always have real-time access to your analytics in the Presenter Hub.
