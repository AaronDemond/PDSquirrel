Upload a PD session (info ▾)

This tab is used to deliver your PD Session to us, for listing on the Site. Fill in the boxes below and select the audio file of your PD Session. Click ‘Upload' to confirm your continuing agreement to our Presenter terms and conditions and deliver your PD Session to us.

At any time after uploading, you may edit the description or subject areas of the session. You also have full control over the comments posted by members on your session. Note that only PD Squirrel members that have taken your PD Session are able to leave comments. At any time, you may instantly delete individual comments or remove and disable all comments entirely.

After uploading, if you need to change the actual title of the session or the audio file, please contact us and we will accommodate you. This is to ensure that we have an opportunity to inform members that have taken the session, as a courtesy to them, if needed in the circumstances.

You may remove your session at any time after uploading, however it remains in the PD library of members that have already taken it. If special circumstances apply, you may contact us at any time so we may assist meeting your needs.
