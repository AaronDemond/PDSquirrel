Record a new session (info ▾)

The PD Squirrel recorder on this page lets you quickly and efficiently record a new session directly from your PC or mobile device. Plug in a USB microphone (we recommend the Snowball Blue microphone as a good choice). Type a name for your recording. Click Record and start talking. Click pause when finished. You may add to the recording by moving the triangle on the play bar to any position, clicking Record and talk more as needed. You may delete portions of the recording by using the trim function – set a start and end point, then click Trim to delete the chosen portion. When you are content with your recording, click Save to keep it, then go to the Upload tab to create the PD session using this recording that you’ve made.

When you first arrive at this page, your browser will ask for permission to use the microphone. Click yes to proceed. This permission is only given for as long as you are on this page. When you leave this Recorder tab and later return, you will need to click yes again. With most browsers you can modify settings to always allow this page to use the microphone, but this is not necessary.
