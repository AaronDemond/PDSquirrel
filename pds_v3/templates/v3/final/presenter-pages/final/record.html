Record a session (info ▾)

Record a PD session (to a maximum of 20 minutes) directly from this page with just your PC and a USB microphone. Our recorder is designed to work primarily with a Google Chrome browser. For best results, we recommend you empty your cache and refresh your browser before starting.

Follow these easy steps. Plug in a USB microphone. Type a name for your recording. Click Record and start talking. Click Pause to take a break or gather your thoughts, then click Record again to resume. Before continuing, move the triangle on the play bar to where you wish to start recording again. At any time, you can listen to your recording by clicking on the play bar. You may delete portions of your recording with our trim function (see below).

When done recording, click Save. Depending on the length, this may take a few minutes. Your recording will then be listed below and ready for you to deliver to us from the ‘Upload' tab.

Depending on your settings, your browser may ask for permission to use the connected microphone once or each time you arrive at this page. Click yes. With most browsers you can modify settings to always allow this page to use the microphone, but this is not necessary.
